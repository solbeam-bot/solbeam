About

An atomic wrapper on Solana for BSV — solBSV.

What

  • Minting is trustless. A BSV light client on Solana verifies deposits. No attestor, no oracle, no committee.
  • Redemption is permissionless and optimistic. Bonded relayers pay BSV and prove the payout on-chain. Fail, and the holder is refunded automatically.
  • No federation. No named signer set, no validator committee, no governance body holding funds.
  • Trading is instant. Pools, P2P atomic swaps and market makers mean only the peg has latency.
